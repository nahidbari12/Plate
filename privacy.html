Plate Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Developer/publisher: 99 Reflections
Privacy/support contact: 99reflectionsapp@gmail.com



What Plate does
Plate is a general wellness food, macro, workout and goal tracker. You can use its ordinary tracking functions without a Plate account or Gemini key. A local profile and username are display information, not an online account.

Data stored on your device
Plate stores the information you enter: name, username, age, fitness goal, height, gender choice, starting/current/goal weight, calorie and macro goals, diary entries, recipes, favorites, weight check-ins, workouts, notes, sets/reps/loads, and earned habit rewards. It also stores workout import preferences and source-app information. These records stay in the app's local storage and are not uploaded to a Plate-operated server. Local app storage is not described as separately encrypted by Plate.

Optional Gemini food-photo analysis
You choose whether to enable this feature and provide your own Google Gemini API key. On this web version your Gemini key stays in memory for the open app session and is excluded from diary backups. It is not saved persistently by Plate. Removing a key from Plate does not revoke it at Google; revoke or rotate it in Google AI Studio if needed.

Only when you choose Analyze, Plate sends the selected food image and food notes directly to Google's Gemini API over HTTPS using your key. It does not attach your profile, weight history or Health Connect records. Google receives the request and ordinary connection metadata. Google's handling and retention depend on your project, service tier and applicable API terms; unpaid service content may be used for product improvement. Review https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms and https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing. Google API limits and charges are the responsibility of the account/project linked to your key.

Plate may use a temporary private cache file for a camera capture. It deletes that working copy after processing, cancellation or cleanup; a photo you already saved in your gallery is not deleted. Selected photo data is processed in memory and is not kept in diary records or exported backups. Nutrition results that you confirm are stored in your local diary.

Optional fitness and nutrition coach
Before sending a question, you choose whether to share your age, height, current/goal weight, fitness goal, configured calorie/macro targets, question and current conversation directly with Google Gemini using your key. Separate optional controls include the last seven days of logged food totals and a summary of manually logged workouts. You can preview the attached profile and summaries. Names, usernames, food names, free-text notes, photos and imported Health Connect records are not automatically attached to coach requests. Questions and your current conversation are sent as entered, so avoid entering secrets or information you do not want to share with Google. Health Connect records are excluded even if the manual-workout summary is selected.

Coach chat is not saved in diary backups or local persistent storage. Clear the conversation to reset the panel. Google may retain or use API submissions under its terms, including product improvement on unpaid services; clearing Plate's panel does not delete Google-held data. The coach offers general adult fitness/nutrition suggestions only, can make mistakes, and does not diagnose, treat or automatically change your targets, meals or workouts.

Barcode lookup
When you scan or type a barcode, Plate sends the product code to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. Their service receives the network request and ordinary connection metadata. Plate does not attach your profile, diary or workout records. See https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use and https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Android version only: optional Health Connect import
With explicit permission, Plate reads exercise sessions and optional active calories, including activity names/types, dates, start/end times, elapsed duration, notes, recording-app identifiers and available energy. It reads the latest 28 days and refreshes when the app is open; it does not continuously read in the background. The data is used to show your workout log, weekly progress and logging rewards. It remains on your device unless you export it yourself.

Plate does not request routes, precise location, heart rate, sleep, medical records or Health Connect write access. Other apps must share exercise sessions through Health Connect for import. Disconnect in Plate to stop imports and revoke Plate's Health Connect permissions, or manage access in Android Health Connect settings. Previously imported records remain local until you hide them, clear app data or uninstall. Hiding an imported record in Plate does not delete it from Health Connect or its original app.

Permissions
Camera access is optional for barcode scanning and taking food photos. You can type a barcode or choose an existing image instead. Android's document picker is used only when you choose a photo or import/export a backup. Health Connect access is optional and limited to the read permissions described above. Plate does not request location or microphone access.

Optional local reminders
Plate can schedule breakfast, lunch, dinner and gentle restart reminders on your device after you opt in. Android 13 and newer also requires notification permission. Reminder preferences, which meals were logged today, the latest food-log activity timestamp, and limited send counters are stored in private app preferences and are used only to decide whether a reminder would be useful. This data is not uploaded to a marketing or analytics server.

Meal reminders are skipped for meals already logged. After two days without logging, routine meal prompts pause; a maximum of two restart check-ins can be sent in the first week, at least three days apart, around 10 a.m. You can disable reminders in Plate or Android notification settings. Phone battery controls, notification settings, time-zone changes and OS scheduling may affect delivery. Local reminder preferences are not included in diary backups.

Backups and deletion
Export is initiated by you and writes a JSON file to the location you select, which may be local or a cloud document provider. Backups include your diary, profile, workouts, recipes and rewards, but not your Gemini key or food photos. Anyone with the backup file can read it; protect it appropriately.

You can edit/delete local entries. To erase all Plate-held data, use Android Settings > Apps > Plate > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall. These actions remove local records and the saved key. Exported backup files and records held by Google, Health Connect or another source app are not deleted automatically; use the relevant provider's controls. Plate has no server account to delete.

Ads, subscriptions and analytics
This release contains no ads, ad SDK, app subscription or developer-operated analytics service. Plate does not sell your personal or Health Connect data or use it for advertising. Platform/provider services may process data under their own terms when you choose the corresponding feature.

Intended audience and wellness limits
Plate is intended for adults and is not designed as a children's service. Nutrition and exercise values can be estimates. Plate is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions.

Changes and contact
Update this policy if app functionality, service providers or data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, contact the developer at the support address above. Requests about provider-held data should also be directed to that provider.
